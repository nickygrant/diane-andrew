## Research and how it informed this design

- [Mandy’s Heavenly Angels](https://www.mandysheavenlyangel.co.uk/): a personal introduction and distinct service routes are useful for a combined medium/Reiki practice. The draft uses its own layout, imagery and text.
- [Ray Hope Reiki & Readings](https://www.rayhope-reiki-readings.co.uk/): visitors need a clear distinction between readings and Reiki, plus locality and a direct enquiry route. No testimonials or health claims were taken from this site.
- [June Greatrex](https://www.junegreatrex.com/): establishes mediumship and Reiki within one practitioner identity. This draft makes the two entrances more prominent.
- [Google Search Central: AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): normal SEO fundamentals also apply to Google's AI search features. Crawlable text, useful internal links, clear factual descriptions and structured data matching visible content matter. There is no special AI schema or required AI text file, and appearance is not guaranteed.
- [Google Search Central: developer guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/get-started-developers): unique titles/descriptions, accessible HTML text, mobile usability and sitemap generation informed the implementation.
- [NCCIH: Reiki](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/reiki): health-related benefits are not established. Copy focuses on the session experience, personal rest and spiritual practice, without claims of curing or treating conditions.
- [International Center for Reiki Training: how a session is given](https://www.reiki.org/faqs/how-reiki-treatment-given): general session mechanics, such as remaining clothed and hands resting lightly on or above the body. Diane should confirm that all suggested practical details match her own practice.
